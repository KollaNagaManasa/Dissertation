Provenance of the documents in this folder
==========================================

The four .md files here are SIMULATED internal templates. They were written by the author for
this dissertation, in a deliberately informal "real team wiki" style, to test whether the
conventions-in-prompt approach survives messier text than the clean conventions in
data/knowledge_base/. They are not exports of any real organisation's documentation, and
names, channels, dates and anecdotes inside them (e.g. "#cloud-platform", "a previous
reviewer") are part of that simulation.

Review status
-------------
- 2026-11-03: technical self-review by the author (automated + manual), with corrections
  applied. Full findings: docs/internal_templates_review.md
- Independent review by the organisation's platform team: NOT YET DONE (reviewer unavailable
  within the project timeline).

This file is .txt on purpose: load_conventions() reads only *.md from this folder into the
`templates` arm's prompt, so provenance notes here do not change what the model sees.
